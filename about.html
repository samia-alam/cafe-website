Our Heritage & Mission

From Origin to Your Cup

A quiet rebellion against rushed mornings. Dedicated to honest sourcing, slow roasts, and true hospitality.

Established 2021

Rooted in Passion, Grown with Heart

The Roasted Bean was conceived around a humble kitchen counter by two college friends who shared a relentless obsession with the subtle floral and chocolate notes of Ethiopian Yirgacheffe and Colombian Huila beans.

Frustrated by commercial coffee chains serving burnt beans in disposable cups, we set out to build something different: a warm, timber-adorned sanctuary where coffee is roasted in small batches, sourdough bread is naturally fermented for 36 hours, and every customer is greeted by their name.

Today, we welcome neighborhood students writing essays, remote software developers sketching code, and families sharing Sunday morning pastries.

What We Believe

Our Guiding Principles

Every choice we make reflects our commitment to sustainability, quality, and human connection.

Direct Trade Sourcing

We skip middlemen and trade directly with smallholder farmers in Guatemala, Kenya, and Sumatra, paying at least 40% above fair trade minimums.

Slow & Mindful Roasting

Our beans are gently drum-roasted on-site in micro-batches under 15 pounds, preserving intricate origin flavors and delicate terroir.

Community First Space

We are a welcoming hub for everyone: fast reliable Wi-Fi, accessible power plugs, student discounts, and outdoor dog-friendly benches.

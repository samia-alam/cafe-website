Our Philosophy

More than just a cup of coffee.

Founded in 2021, The Roasted Bean began as an intimate haven for thinkers, students, and neighborhood neighbors. We believe in direct trade relationships with sustainable coffee farms worldwide.

Whether you need a serene nook with electrical outlets to finish your dissertation, or an uplifting meeting space with friends, our doors and patio are always warm.
